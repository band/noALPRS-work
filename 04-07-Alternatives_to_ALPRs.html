Alternatives to ALPRs

  • Independent research does not show that ALPRs are effective:
    • A 2019 LSU case study investigating the use of Baton Rouge PD’s use of ALPRs found that ALPRs showed no statistical reduction in general or specific crime.
  • Research showing that making non-police investments decreases rates of harm:
    • Research by Sara Heller of the University of Pennsylvania found that a summer jobs program significantly reduced violent crime by teenagers for as long as a year after they stopped working and returned to school. https://www.science.org/content/article/summer-jobs-lower-violent-crime-rate-urban-teens
  • Specific program alternatives:
    • ATX Peace
    • Data shows Travis County jail diversion program reduces arrests, mental health crises - https://share.google/baQgvsy9S9FDk1GdY
