# No ALPRs Toolkit

*“No system of mass surveillance has existed in any society that we know of to this point that has not been abused.”* \-Edward Snowden

According to the [Wikipedia article on mass surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_surveillance), it is the single most indicative distinguishing trait of totalitarian regimes. 

In the wake of a shooting made possible by the negligence of a gun shop owner and a refusal by politicians to enact common sense gun regulation (see [here](?tab=t.0) for more info), Austin Mayor Kirk Watson is throwing his hands up and signalling an eagerness to trade our civil liberties for the false promise of security by reinstating Austin’s ALPR (Automated License Plate Reader) program. We are creating this toolkit to educate our neighbors with accurate information, equip our allies to advocate against mass surveillance, and offer a tool that can be easily shared with community members to mobilize in opposition to ALPRs and in support of real solutions to public safety issues. 

**Call To Action:**

1. Use the scripts at the bottom of this page to email and call your City Councilmember, urging them to (1) hold firm against ALPRs and AI mass surveillance and (2) demand investments that prevent harm before it occurs.   
   1. Contact City Council \- use [this link](https://www.austintexas.gov/council) to find your Councilmember’s email & phone number, or use [this link](https://www.austintexas.gov/email/all-council-members) to email everyone on City Council at once.   
2. Save this toolkit and circle back for information on how to register to testify (or share your email via [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdBITKqhgKvJ-XnfqJjUub_IfPdN_ZSu-iDj6HIE7rzkdK60w/viewform?usp=publish-editor) to be notified when ALPRs are coming back). While we expect an ALPR proposal to be voted on later, we don’t know exactly when. We will automatically update this toolkit when timeline information becomes available, and it would be incredibly powerful to have folks sign up to testify in opposition to ALPRs.   
3. Find at least three people to share this toolkit with. The opposition is mobilizing in full force \- if we show how widely and deeply felt the opposition is to tech surveillance and ALPRs is in our community, we can beat these tech companies, fight for real safety, and defend democracy for our descendants. 

Script:

*“I’m reaching out to urge the councilmember to stand firm against Automated License Plate Readers. ALPRs are being used to deport our immigrant neighbors, target abortion-seekers and activists, and intimidate people engaged in lawful ICE watch activity. Erroneous reads from ALPRs have led to numerous wrongful arrests, particularly of Black people and their children, resulting in millions of tax dollars being paid out in legal settlements to impacted families. There have been many stories of police abusing their access to ALPRs to stalk ex-partners or spouses or to carry out personal vendettas that have nothing to do with law enforcement. We do not accept our tax dollars being used to pay for the privilege of these harms, especially when there is no research or evidence showing ALPRs impact crime rates or effectively stop crimes. We demand our limited resources be invested wisely into proven strategies that prevent harm before it occurs.”*
