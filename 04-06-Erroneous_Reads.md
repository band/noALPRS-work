**Arguments Against ALPRs Related to Erroneous Readings**

* One study found [Flock ALPRs misread 1 in 10 license plates](https://centralcurrent.org/a-study-found-syracuses-new-license-plate-readers-make-frequent-mistakes-councilors-didnt-know-before-approving-them/).   
* An erroneous ALPR reading in Aurora, CO led police to pull over a Black woman who they wrongly believed had stolen a car, [forcing her and four children aged 17, 14, 12, and 6 to the concrete at gunpoint](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2020/aug/04/colorado-police-draw-guns-mistaken-stolen-car-stop-video-black-woman-brittney-gilliam).   
  * The eventual settlement of a lawsuit related to this error included a [$1.9M payout of tax dollars](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2024/feb/06/aurora-settlement-black-family-held-gunpoint-police) to the impacted family.   
* A Black man was wrongly pulled over in Toledo, OH and was [violently attacked by a police dog due to an ALPR misread](https://www.businessinsider.com/flock-safety-alpr-cameras-misreads-2026-3) that led police to believe he was driving a stolen vehicle.   
  * The city settlement with the victim in this case was [$35,000 in tax dollars](https://www.wtol.com/article/news/local/city-of-toledo-to-pay-brandon-upchurch-35000-dollar-settlement-in-k9-attack-traffic-stop-local-news/512-e6ad4d91-75de-4bc7-a088-5aebbdd0907e).   
* A Denver, CO woman was wrongly accused of package theft by police using ALPRs and [had to subsequently prove her own innocence](https://coloradosun.com/2025/10/28/flock-camera-police-colorado-columbine-valley/).   
* A woman in Detroit, MI was [wrongly detained with her toddler](https://www.freep.com/story/news/local/michigan/detroit/2025/04/02/detroit-settles-lawsuit-license-plate-reader-error-isoke-robinson/82753538007/?gnt-cfr=1&gca-cat=p&gca-uir=false&gca-epti=undefined&gca-ft=0&gca-ds=sophi) after Detroit wrongly linked her car to a shooting due to their use of ALPRs. The city paid out a $35,000 settlement of tax dollars to the victim.   
* A pair of Hispanic men in San Diego were wrongly linked to a violent crime by police incorrectly interpreting an ALPR ping, leading one of them to be jailed for a month and miss Thanksgiving with his family. [https://timesofsandiego.com/crime/2026/06/07/a-flock-license-plate-reader-linked-a-san-diego-man-to-a-violent-crime-he-was-five-miles-away/](https://timesofsandiego.com/crime/2026/06/07/a-flock-license-plate-reader-linked-a-san-diego-man-to-a-violent-crime-he-was-five-miles-away/)   
* A Black woman in San Francisco was ordered by police to kneel in handcuffs after [ALPRs led them to wrongly believe she was driving a stolen vehicle](https://www.sfexaminer.com/news/city-set-to-approve-wrongful-arrest-suit-settlement/article_2606ea3a-bd84-56b1-be3f-e6d608c397ca.html). The settlement in this case was $495,000.   
* An ALPR misread in Atherton, CA led police to [arrest a man they wrongly believed had stolen a car](https://www.almanacnews.com/police/2024/02/12/atherton-officer-involved-in-the-arrest-of-m-a-student-also-named-in-lawsuit-over-unreasonable-seizure-and-use-of-excessive-force/). They held him at gunpoint and ordered him to lie on his stomach to be handcuffed.   
* Two sisters in Espanola, NM were pulled over and held at gunpoint, also because an ALPR misread led police to believe they were in a stolen vehicle. The younger sister, [age 12, was handcuffed and put in the back of a police car](https://www.santafenewmexican.com/news/local_news/sisters-sue-espa-ola-over-traffic-stop-they-say-was-illegal/article_1c949c30-ae3e-11ee-ad85-f71a8c9ecb1e.html).
