Personalize Your Script + More Information on ALPRs

In this section, you can take the below template and use it to create a more personalized script for calling your City Councilmember. The following information also outlines some of the most egregious harms from ALPRs, with relevant links and resources, for further education.

This template can also be made longer and used as a testimony in front of City Council. Testimony is limited to 2 minutes / around 260-300 words. Testimony can be delivered in-person or virtually over the phone (virtual testimony requires registration about one day in advance). See tab “No ALPRs Toolkit” (call to action item #2) for instructions on how to register to testify when that information becomes available.

INSTRUCTIONS: Go to File > Make A Copy > click the “Make A Copy” button to get started.
