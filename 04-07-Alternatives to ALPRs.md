**Alternatives to ALPRs**

* Independent research does not show that ALPRs are effective:  
  * A 2019 LSU case study investigating the use of Baton Rouge PD’s use of ALPRs found that [ALPRs showed no statistical reduction in general or specific crime](https://www.lsu.edu/hss/sociology/research/CAPER/CAPER_Fact_Sheets/fs18.pdf).   
* Research showing that making non-police investments decreases rates of harm:  
  * Research by Sara Heller of the University of Pennsylvania found that a summer jobs program significantly reduced violent crime by teenagers for as long as a year after they stopped working and returned to school. [https://www.science.org/content/article/summer-jobs-lower-violent-crime-rate-urban-teens](https://www.science.org/content/article/summer-jobs-lower-violent-crime-rate-urban-teens)   
* Specific program alternatives:  
  * [ATX Peace](https://www.atxpeace.org/) \- a collaboration between Life Anew (a restorative justice organization) and Jail To Jobs (which reduces recidivism by supporting folks exiting incarceration with employment and economic stability). ATX Peace builds deep relationships in communities impacted by violence in order to learn about potential escalation before it happens, allowing them to intervene and offer alternatives to violence. In the Community Violence Intervention space nationally, ATX Peace is likely one of the 10 best programs in the country modeling this approach and contributing to research showing its efficacy.   
  * Data shows Travis County jail diversion program reduces arrests, mental health crises \- [https://share.google/baQgvsy9S9FDk1GdY](https://share.google/baQgvsy9S9FDk1GdY)
