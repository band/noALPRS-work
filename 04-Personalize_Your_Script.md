This template can also be made longer and used as a testimony in front of City Council. Testimony is limited to 2 minutes / around 260-300 words. Testimony can be delivered in-person or virtually over the phone (virtual testimony requires registration about one day in advance). See tab “[No ALPRs Toolkit](?tab=t.hcih3zhvty8y)” (call to action item \#2) for instructions on how to register to testify when that information becomes available.
