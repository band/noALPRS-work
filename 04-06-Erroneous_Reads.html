Arguments Against ALPRs Related to Erroneous Readings

  • One study found Flock ALPRs misread 1 in 10 license plates.
  • An erroneous ALPR reading in Aurora, CO led police to pull over a Black woman who they wrongly believed had stolen a car, forcing her and four children aged 17, 14, 12, and 6 to the concrete at gunpoint.
    • The eventual settlement of a lawsuit related to this error included a $1.9M payout of tax dollars to the impacted family.
  • A Black man was wrongly pulled over in Toledo, OH and was violently attacked by a police dog due to an ALPR misread that led police to believe he was driving a stolen vehicle.
    • The city settlement with the victim in this case was $35,000 in tax dollars.
  • A Denver, CO woman was wrongly accused of package theft by police using ALPRs and had to subsequently prove her own innocence.
  • A woman in Detroit, MI was wrongly detained with her toddler after Detroit wrongly linked her car to a shooting due to their use of ALPRs. The city paid out a $35,000 settlement of tax dollars to the victim.
  • A pair of Hispanic men in San Diego were wrongly linked to a violent crime by police incorrectly interpreting an ALPR ping, leading one of them to be jailed for a month and miss Thanksgiving with his family. https://timesofsandiego.com/crime/2026/06/07/a-flock-license-plate-reader-linked-a-san-diego-man-to-a-violent-crime-he-was-five-miles-away/
  • A Black woman in San Francisco was ordered by police to kneel in handcuffs after ALPRs led them to wrongly believe she was driving a stolen vehicle. The settlement in this case was $495,000.
  • An ALPR misread in Atherton, CA led police to arrest a man they wrongly believed had stolen a car. They held him at gunpoint and ordered him to lie on his stomach to be handcuffed.
  • Two sisters in Espanola, NM were pulled over and held at gunpoint, also because an ALPR misread led police to believe they were in a stolen vehicle. The younger sister, age 12, was handcuffed and put in the back of a police car.
  • Additional stories regarding ALPR misreads detailed here.
