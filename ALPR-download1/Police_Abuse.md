**Police Abuse Arguments Against ALPRs**

* Police have repeatedly abused their access to ALPRs to stalk their partners or ex-partners.   
  * [Police Family Violence Fact Sheet](https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2017R1/Downloads/CommitteeMeetingDocument/132808) \- details research from the early 1990s investigating the prevalence of domestic violence in police families.   
  * In 2024, the former police chief of Sedgwick, Kansas [abused ALPRs](https://local12.com/news/nation-world/police-chief-gets-caught-using-license-plate-cameras-to-track-his-ex-girlfriend-228-times-arrests-charges-probation-flock-safety-follow-stalk-new-boyfriend-broke-up-out-of-town-misuse) to stalk his ex-girlfriend, making 228 searches of her vehicles over four months and following her and her new partner in his police cruiser.   
  * Another 2024 case out of North Charleston, South Carolina involved a police lieutenant who was merely demoted after [using ALPRs to stalk his wife](https://www.kcrg.com/video/2024/06/24/video-north-charleston-police-officer-demoted-using-cameras-track-wife/), who he suspected of having an affair.   
  * In 2025, [a story broke](https://www.miamiherald.com/news/state/florida/article299881644.html) detailing a Florida police officer's frightening pattern of stalking, control, and abuse of his then-girlfriend using ALPRs, executing over a hundred illegal searches.   
  * In 2026, a Milwaukee police officer was investigated for [abusing ALPR access to stalk his ex-partner](https://wisconsinexaminer.com/2026/02/26/milwaukee-officer-accused-of-misusing-flock-surveillance-cameras/). The story references several other instances of police abuse.   
* Police have repeatedly abused their access to ALPRs to target individuals over personal vendettas that have nothing to do with law enforcement.   
  * A story broke in 2025 about a police chief in the Atlanta suburbs who [abused ALPRs to stalk and harass](https://apnews.com/article/georgia-plate-readers-stalk-harass-chief-arrested-39adb6f89fc2074da61f2801fef3f180) multiple people who were not being investigated in relation to crimes.   
* Police used ALPRs to search for Romani people, [using racist terms to target people in this ethnic group](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/11/license-plate-surveillance-logs-reveal-racist-policing-against-romani-people) in violation of stated policies asserting ethnicity or race should play no role in the investigation or prosecution of crimes.  
* Police abuse of ALPRs is expensive, costing municipalities millions of dollars in legal settlements.
