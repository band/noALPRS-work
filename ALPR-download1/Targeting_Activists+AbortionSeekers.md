**Arguments Against ALPRs Related to Targeting of Activists & Abortion-Seekers**

* Abortion-seekers:  
  * A [Dallas Observer](https://www.dallasobserver.com/news/dallas-police-cameras-used-in-murder-investigation-after-abortion-40604138/) story details a 2025 Johnson County case in which a woman self-managed an abortion, Johnson County police searched for her using ALPR data with intent to prosecute her in relation to her abortion, and then attempted to cover their tracks with falsified police reports. The woman was a domestic abuse victim who in [this detailed post](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2025/10/flock-safety-and-texas-sheriff-claimed-license-plate-search-was-missing-person-it) from the Electronic Frontier Foundation alleges that her abuser choked her, held her at gunpoint, and made her beg for her life on the day she took the abortion medication.   
* Activists:  
  * The Trump administration has openly stated its intent to target anti-ICE protestors as well as anyone they can label domestic terrorists on the basis of expressing “anti-Americanism, anti-capitalism, and anti-Christianity” in their views, with ICE acquisition of numerous surveillance technology laid out as foundational to their strategy in this targeting. [ALPRs are one of many tools being acquired with intent to deploy for this purpose](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/ice-wants-go-after-dissenters-well-immigrants).   
  * De-Flock is an open-source developer mapping the locations of Flock ALPRs, offering information that should be available to the public. Flock has [threatened DeFlock with lawsuits](https://www.404media.co/flock-threatens-open-source-developer-mapping-its-surveillance-cameras/) and called them terrorists.
