Privacy Arguments Against ALPRs

  • ALPRs are not just harvesting our license plate numbers - they are collecting large troves of data and information about us without our informed consent.
    • Flock feeds data fusion tools which can build up profiles using public records and their social media usage. This tool also utilizes AI to flag certain drivers as suspicious based on driving patterns. These companies are feeding into these tools that are tracking everything about us, without our consent.
  • ALPRs are invasive tech that gives people we don’t know access to live streams of sensitive areas.
    • In Dunwoody, GA, ALPR executives accessed footage of children in a gymnasium without their parents’ permission for a sales pitch to police.
  • Flock ALPRs use AI to determine whether individual behavior patterns are “suspicious”, meaning you could be targeted based on an algorithm rather than actually engaging in illegal behavior.
