According to Texas law, guns may not be legally sold to underage persons, but **the gun shop failed to ID the teenagers prior to putting the deadly weapon in their hands.**   
  * One of the suspects had a [warrant](https://krcgtv.com/news/nation-world/austin-police-searching-for-suspect-in-connection-to-multiple-shootings-on-saturday) out for his arrest, which APD failed to execute, prior to the shooting.

They claimed ALPRs “would have helped” and signaled an intention to bring ALPRs back to Austin.

By the end of 2025, all Flock cameras had been removed from city property. The talking points used by APD and the Mayor are based on a lie.
